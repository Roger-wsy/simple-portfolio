# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Roger Wong Sie Yang for software engineering opportunities. They need to understand his current role, career progression, technical range, and evidence of production work quickly enough to decide whether to review his resume or contact him.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Roger's professional profile, experience, selected projects, skills, education, and contact details. Success means a qualified recruiter or hiring manager can confidently assess his fit, download his resume, and start a conversation.

## Positioning

Roger is a full-stack software engineer currently building AI-assisted maritime compliance systems at Athena Dynamics. His experience connects agent-oriented AI workflows and enterprise web systems with earlier production work across fintech, mobile applications, cloud infrastructure, and regulated domains.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from a resume, LinkedIn, GitHub, or a direct application. They may scan the portfolio on desktop or mobile and need direct access to the resume, professional profiles, project evidence, and contact details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a responsive React and Vite single-page portfolio.
- It includes anchored navigation, a light and dark appearance, resume download, email contact, and external LinkedIn, GitHub, and project links.
- Athena Dynamics is the current role and iFAST is historical experience.
- Singapore is the current location.
- Existing factual career content must be preserved unless Roger supplies an update.
- Experience, achievements, statistics, clients, or projects must never be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Roger Wong Sie Yang.
- The portfolio should be professional, direct, technically credible, and personal rather than promotional.
- Orbital mechanics is a binding conceptual metaphor for the experience, used to express career trajectory and connected systems rather than as a decorative space theme.

## Evidence on Hand

- Structured profile, experience, project, skills, education, leadership, and contact content in `src/data/portfolio.js`.
- Current resume at `public/resume-roger-wong-2026.pdf`.
- Existing orbital implementation in `src/versions/RevampPortfolio.jsx` is evidence of prior intent but not visual authority for this redesign.
- No testimonials, client endorsements, awards, or additional case-study media are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Establish Roger's present role and professional direction before showing supporting detail.
2. Make career progression and evidence easier to understand than a generic technology inventory.
3. Let verified work and specific responsibilities carry credibility instead of marketing claims.
4. Keep resume review and direct contact obvious without turning the site into a conversion funnel.
5. Preserve accessibility, performance, and clarity while creating a memorable portfolio experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic HTML, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, touch input, and reduced-motion preferences.
